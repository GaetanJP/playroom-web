The games

Four games. Every kind of night.

Loud and fast, sneaky, or a little too honest. Pick one for the room you're in.

Guessing · Teams4–16 players

Fishbowl

Describe it, say one word, then act it out. Three rounds, one pile of clues, and the same words come back every time.

Deception3–12 players

The Outsider

Everyone knows the secret word but one. Give a clue, vote, and catch them before they bluff their way through.

Opinions2–12 players

Hot Take

One player secretly rates a spicy take from 1 to 10. Everyone else guesses how hard they agree.

Mind-meld2–12 players

Same Brain

Everyone answers the same prompt. Write what the room will write, and score for every mind you match.

All four games are free to play, with no ads.

How it works

From couch to game in under a minute.

  1. +

    Start a room

    One person opens Playroom and taps Start. They're the host.

  2. BLMKR

    Share the code

    Read out the 5-letter code, or send the invite link. Friends type it in and they're in.

  3. Everyone plays

    Secret words, votes and answers land on each person's own phone. Nobody peeks.

  4. ✓ No accounts

    Nothing to sign up for

    Pick a nickname and a colour. That's the whole setup, for the host and for every guest.
