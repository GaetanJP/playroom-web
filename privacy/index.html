Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

The short version
  • You don't need an account. Playroom gives your device an anonymous guest id.
  • Email is optional, and only used to save and restore your Party Pass.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no ads. We don't sell or share your data.
  • Rooms and everything said in them are deleted automatically.

This policy covers the Playroom - Party Games app for iPhone and iPad (bundle id com.byld.playroom) and this website. Playroom is made by BYLD ("we", "us"). Questions go to byldforfun@gmail.com.

What the app collects

An anonymous guest id

The first time you open Playroom, it signs you in anonymously and creates a random user id. It isn't linked to your name, your Apple ID, your phone number or your contacts. It's stored on your device so you stay the same player between games, and on our server so we can keep rooms and purchases straight. "Reset this device" in Settings removes it from your device.

What you type into a room

When you start or join a room we store the nickname and colour you choose, and what you enter while you play: Fishbowl words, clues, votes, ratings and answers. Other players in that room see this, as the games require. It is not used for anything else.

Your email, only if you choose to add one

You can add an email in Settings → Party Pass so a purchase survives a lost or reset phone. We use it to send you one-time sign-in codes and to restore your pass. We don't send marketing to it and don't share it.

Purchase records

If you buy Party Pass, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card or billing details. Apple and our purchase-verification provider, RevenueCat, tell us that a purchase happened, and we store a record linked to your user id: the product, the store, whether it was a test (sandbox) or real purchase, the purchase date and when we verified it. That record is what unlocks Party Pass for you and for the rooms you host.

Short-lived abuse protection

To stop spam and abuse, the server notes how often a user id performs actions like creating rooms. These notes are deleted after a day.

What we don't do

  • No tracking. Playroom doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and doesn't use the advertising identifier.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No access to your contacts, location, photos or microphone.
  • We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't use it for advertising.

How long we keep it

  • Rooms, including nicknames and everything entered during games, are cleaned up automatically: a room is deleted a few hours after its last activity (currently 6 hours), and within minutes after it's closed. Finished game state is cleared sooner.
  • Abuse-protection notes are deleted after one day.
  • Your guest id, optional email and purchase record are kept so your Party Pass keeps working, until you ask us to delete them.
  • Website sign-ups are kept until you ask to be removed, or until we stop sending updates.

Who processes data for us

We use a small number of service providers, only to run Playroom:

  • Supabase hosts our database, sign-in (including sending sign-in code emails) and server functions.
  • RevenueCat verifies App Store purchases.
  • Apple processes App Store payments under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Google stores website sign-up emails in a Google Sheet, and serves this website's fonts.

Like any internet service, these providers receive technical data such as IP addresses when your device connects to them, and may keep it briefly in their operational logs.

This website

This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. If you sign up for updates, we store your email address, the time, and the page you signed up from in a Google Sheet that only we can access. We use it to tell you when Playroom launches and when new packs arrive. Every email can be answered with "remove me", or write to us, and we'll delete it.

The support form doesn't send anything itself: it opens your own mail app with a message addressed to us.

Children

Playroom is a party game for groups and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from them. The After Dark pack is for adults 18 and over. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Play without giving us anything. No account, no email, no real name needed.
  • Access or delete your data. Email us, from the address you added in the app if you added one, and we'll tell you what we hold or delete it. If you never added an email, we have nothing that identifies you; "Reset this device" in Settings disconnects your device from its guest id, and rooms expire on their own. Deleting a purchase record removes Party Pass from that user id; Apple keeps its own record of the purchase, so Restore Purchases still works with the same Apple ID.
  • Leave the update list at any time by replying to any email or writing to us.

Depending on where you live (for example under Canada's PIPEDA, the GDPR in Europe or state laws in the US), you may have further rights to access, correct, port or delete your data, or to complain to a data-protection authority. We'll honour those requests.

Security and storage

Data is sent over encrypted connections. The database enforces per-user access rules: other players can only see what you share in a room you're both in, and only the server can grant Party Pass. Our providers may store and process data outside your country.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and for significant changes we'll say so in the app or on this site.
