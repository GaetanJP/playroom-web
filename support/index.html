Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open Playroom, pick a nickname and a colour, and you're playing. The app gives your phone an anonymous guest id behind the scenes. The only time an email helps is to keep a Party Pass safe if you lose or reset your phone.

How do my friends join my room?

Everyone needs Playroom on their own phone. Start a room and read out the 5-letter code, or share the invite link. Friends tap Join and type the code. Codes only use consonants, so there's no mixing up O and 0.

"Room not found" when I type a code

Check the letters with the host. Rooms close when the host ends them, and empty or idle rooms are cleaned up automatically after a few hours, so an old code won't work. The host can start a new room in seconds.

Someone dropped out or their phone locked

Reopen Playroom and rejoin with the same code: you'll get your seat back. Keeping the screen on during a game helps.

How many players can play?
  • Fishbowl: 4–16, in teams
  • The Outsider: 3–12
  • Hot Take: 2–12
  • Same Brain: 2–12
What does Party Pass include?

A one-time purchase (no subscription) that unlocks the Deep Cuts and After Dark (18+) packs, longer games, and the Fishbowl Sound Effects round. All four games stay free without it. More about Party Pass.

My friends don't see the Party Pass content

Party Pass follows the host. Everyone gets it in rooms you host. If a friend without the pass starts the room, it plays with the free content. Start the room from the phone that bought the pass.

I got a new phone or reset mine. How do I get Party Pass back?

Two ways, both in Settings → Party Pass:

  • Restore purchases works with the same Apple ID you bought it with.
  • Sign in with your email, if you added one, using the one-time code we send you.

Still missing? Email us with roughly when you bought it.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Rooms and everything said in them are deleted automatically. To delete your email or purchase record, write to us from the email you added in the app. Details are in the privacy policy.

A prompt or word is offensive or wrong

Tell us which game and pack, and the text if you can. We fix content quickly.
